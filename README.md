Vitiligo GWAS Analysis

Introductory bioinformatics project in R built to analyze genetic risk associated with vitiligo using public Genome-Wide Associated Study (GWAS) data principles and Hardy-Weinberg Equilibrium (HWE) modeling.

Overview:
Vitiligo is a complex autoimmune condition characterized by the loss of skin pigmentation in certain areas. Genetic risk or predispotion to vitiligo is typically linked to key pigment involved genes, such as the TYR (tyrosinase) gene. This model simply automates population genetics calculations to model expected genotype frequencies, and evaluate allele risk distributions. I have been affected by vitiligo, albeit only slightly on my eyebrows, for almost 10 years, and was inspired to dive deeper into the genetics behind it, starting with this simple project used to map out the expected genotype frequencies.
